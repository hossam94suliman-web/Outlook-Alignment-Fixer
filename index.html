    What this does:
    Rebuilds common email layout patterns so they render correctly in Outlook Desktop (Classic) — the trickiest email client to support. It fixes fluid multi-column layouts, div padding and centering, spacer rows, bullet lists,buttons, background images, icon rows, and image sizing/centering, all while keeping your design responsive on every other client.
    

    
    What it won't fix:
    CSS flex/grid/float/position, and anything else it can't safely rebuild — these get flagged in the Changes Log for you to review and fix by hand.
    

    
    Good to know:
    Every fix is listed in the Changes Log tab, and nothing is uploaded — everything runs locally in your browser.
